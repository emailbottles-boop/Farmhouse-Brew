Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

What we collect

The only information this site collects is what you type into the contact form on the homepage, if you choose to use it:

  • Name — required
  • Email address — required, so we can reply
  • Phone number — optional
  • Your message — required

That information is stored in our Firebase database so the owner can read and respond to it. It is not shared, sold, or used for marketing.

What we don't collect

This site has no user accounts, no analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising pixels, and no mailing list. We do not use cookies to track you across visits. If you never submit the contact form, we collect nothing about you at all.

Third-party services

A small number of outside services are used to run this page, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site.
  • Google Maps — the embedded map on the Hours & Location section.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud) — stores the menu, hours, and contact form submissions, and handles the owner's login to the admin dashboard.

We don't control what these providers do with technical data (like your IP address) needed to load their content in your browser.

Requesting your data be removed

If you'd like a message you submitted through the contact form deleted, get in touch through the contact form with the email address you used, and we'll remove it from our records by hand. There is currently no phone number listed for the stand — the contact form is the way to reach us.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects changes, this page will be updated to match.
